# Ordering Process — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## How to Place an Order
Ordering is entirely self-service on our website, in five steps you move through one after another: start by browsing our collections on the homepage (Birthday, Wedding, Baby Shower, Graduation, or Corporate) and choosing a cake template from the collection you like; continue to the Designer to customize it; the next step is reviewing your order; and the final step is entering your contact information (name, phone, email, and any notes — such as a dietary or religious requirement) to confirm. If you're not sure what to do next at any point, the page you're on will guide you to the next step — you'll receive an order number on the confirmation page once your order is placed.

## Customizing Your Cake
Customization happens in the Designer step, right after you pick a template: choose the cake size, flavor (Vanilla, Chocolate, or Red Velvet sponge), filling (Strawberry, Chocolate Ganache, or Lemon Curd), and frosting (Buttercream, Cream Cheese, or Dark Chocolate Ganache). The collection/occasion itself (e.g. Wedding vs. Birthday) is set by which template you started from — to design a cake for a different occasion, start again from a different collection on the homepage. Changes to flavor, filling, or frosting after an order is confirmed are usually possible up to 48 hours before pickup; size or design changes closer to the date may not be, since production may already be underway (see Production Workflow, and the Customer Service Handbook's "Can I change my order?" guidance).

## Questions While Ordering
You don't need to finish — or even start — an order to ask a question. Our website chat and WhatsApp are both available at any point, before, during, or after ordering, for questions about ingredients, allergies, dietary or religious requirements, pricing, or anything else about a cake.

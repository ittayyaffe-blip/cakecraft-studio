# Frequently Asked Questions — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## How far in advance do I need to order?
It depends on the collection: Birthday, Baby Shower, and Graduation cakes need 2–14 days; Corporate orders need 3–10 days; Wedding cakes need 14–45 days. See the Bakery Operations Manual and Wedding Cake Guide for why weddings need so much more lead time.

## Can I get a cake with no nuts, no gluten, or no dairy?
We can omit specific ingredients like a nut garnish, but we cannot guarantee an allergen-free product of any kind, because our kitchen is a single shared space. Customers with a severe allergy should be told this clearly rather than given a false assurance. See the Allergen Policy.

## What flavors do you offer?
Vanilla, Chocolate, and Red Velvet sponge; Strawberry, Chocolate Ganache, or Lemon Curd filling; Buttercream, Cream Cheese, or Dark Chocolate Ganache frosting. Any combination is available across all five collections — see the Recipe Guide for pairing recommendations.

## Do you deliver?
Yes, within 25 km of the bakery, booked at least 48 hours ahead in 2-hour windows. Wedding and multi-tier Corporate orders include delivery and on-site setup as part of the order. See the Delivery Policy.

## What if I'm late picking up my cake?
We hold it refrigerated and will try to reach you by phone. If it's not collected within 48 hours with no response, the order is marked as a no-show and is not eligible for a refund. See the Pickup Policy.

## Can I change my order after confirming it?
Flavor, filling, and frosting changes are usually fine up to 48 hours before pickup. Size or design changes closer to the date may not be possible since production may already be underway. See Production Workflow.

## Do you require a deposit?
Only for Wedding orders — a non-refundable 30% deposit at design confirmation. Every other collection is paid in full at confirmation or pickup/delivery. See the Pricing Policy.

## Can you put our company logo on a cake?
Yes, this is common for Corporate orders — via an edible print or a hand-piped color match. See the Corporate Orders Guide.

## What's your cancellation policy?
Full refund more than 7 days out, except the non-refundable Wedding deposit. 50% refund inside 7 days. No refund for a no-show. See the Pricing Policy.

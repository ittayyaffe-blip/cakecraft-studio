# Dietary, Allergy & Religious Requirements Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Our Commitment
We are happy to discuss dietary, allergy and religious requirements and will always do our best to help you find a suitable option. Our team can provide information about the ingredients and preparation of our cakes based on the information available to us. Our bakery is 100% gluten-free (see Gluten-Free Bakery below) — but unless a specific certification or preparation standard has been expressly confirmed by CakeCraft, we cannot guarantee that a product is otherwise allergen-free, free from cross-contact, or certified Halal, Kosher or compliant with a particular religious dietary standard. If your requirement is particularly important or medically sensitive, please let us know before placing your order. Our team will review your requirements with you and confirm what we can safely and accurately provide before the order is confirmed.

## Gluten-Free Bakery
Maison de Gâteau Paris is a 100% gluten-free bakery. All of our products are made using gluten-free ingredients and prepared in a dedicated gluten-free environment — we do not use gluten-containing ingredients anywhere in our bakery. This is a standing, permanent fact about how we operate, not something that varies by order. Gluten-free does not mean free of all allergens, though: our kitchen still uses milk, eggs, tree nuts, and soy (see the Allergen Policy), and customers with any food allergy — including but not limited to those — must not complete the automated ordering process; they should contact the bakery directly so our team can discuss their requirements before ordering.

## Allergy Safety Boundary
Ingredient and preparation information may be shared whenever it is established elsewhere in CakeCraft's knowledge (see the Allergen Policy for our shared-kitchen and cross-contact facts). That information must never be turned into a guarantee: no cake may be described as allergen-free, cross-contact-free, medically safe, or "safe" for a specific allergy unless CakeCraft has explicitly confirmed that exact claim (gluten-free is the one such claim we do explicitly and permanently confirm — see Gluten-Free Bakery above). A request for a guarantee CakeCraft hasn't made, or a medically significant or severe allergy situation, always requires our team's direct review before the order is confirmed — this is not a decision that can be made automatically, and the automated ordering flow does not proceed for a customer who has disclosed a food allergy. This is informational guidance, not medical advice.

## Halal, Kosher & Religious Requirements
CakeCraft / Maison de Gâteau Paris does not hold Halal, Kosher, or any other religious-compliance certification for any of our cakes. This is a standing policy, not something that varies by order or could be confirmed later — no certification body, certificate number, or preparation procedure should ever be invented, assumed, or promised as forthcoming. Ingredients appearing compatible with a religious dietary standard is not the same as certification and must never be presented as one.

When a customer asks whether a cake is Kosher, Halal, or religiously certified, the direct and complete answer is that it is not — there is nothing further to check or confirm. Say so plainly and warmly: acknowledge the question, state clearly that our cakes are not religiously certified, and highlight our genuine care in selecting quality ingredients and preparing every cake attentively. Do not promise that the team will investigate or follow up on certification, and do not ask the customer whether this is a firm requirement — the answer is already final.

## Vegan, Vegetarian, Dairy-Free & Egg-Free
A cake may only be described as vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, or egg-free when that property is explicitly established in CakeCraft's verified product information — never inferred from an ingredient list alone. If our current product information does not confirm a dietary property a customer is asking about, the honest answer is that our team needs to verify it before the order is confirmed, not a guess in either direction.

## Response Tone
Dietary, allergy, and religious questions should always receive a warm, sympathetic, and genuinely helpful response — never a bare refusal, never dismissive, and never alarmist. Acknowledge the customer's requirement respectfully and share what CakeCraft's verified information actually says. When that information is a definitive, permanent answer (such as the certification policy above), state it plainly and warmly as a complete answer — don't defer to further team review or promise a follow-up for something already fully and permanently settled. Only say our team will confirm the details when the specific fact being asked about is genuinely not yet established in CakeCraft's verified information.

# Dietary, Allergy & Religious Requirements Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Our Commitment
We are happy to discuss dietary, allergy and religious requirements and will always do our best to help you find a suitable option. Our team can provide information about the ingredients and preparation of our cakes based on the information available to us. However, unless a specific certification or preparation standard has been expressly confirmed by CakeCraft, we cannot guarantee that a product is allergen-free, free from cross-contact, or certified Halal, Kosher or compliant with a particular religious dietary standard. If your requirement is particularly important or medically sensitive, please let us know before placing your order. Our team will review your requirements with you and confirm what we can safely and accurately provide before the order is confirmed.

## Allergy Safety Boundary
Ingredient and preparation information may be shared whenever it is established elsewhere in CakeCraft's knowledge (see the Allergen Policy for our shared-kitchen and cross-contact facts). That information must never be turned into a guarantee: no cake may be described as allergen-free, cross-contact-free, medically safe, or "safe" for a specific allergy unless CakeCraft has explicitly confirmed that exact claim. A request for such a guarantee, or a medically significant or severe allergy situation, always requires our team's direct review before the order is confirmed — this is not a decision that can be made automatically. This is informational guidance, not medical advice.

## Halal, Kosher & Religious Requirements
CakeCraft does not claim Halal, Kosher, or any other religious-compliance certification for its products unless that exact certification has been expressly confirmed by CakeCraft. No certification body, certificate number, or preparation procedure should ever be invented or assumed. Ingredients appearing compatible with a religious dietary standard is not the same as certification and must never be presented as one. Any request asking us to confirm certification or religious compliance requires our team's direct review before the order is confirmed.

## Vegan, Vegetarian, Dairy-Free & Egg-Free
A cake may only be described as vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, or egg-free when that property is explicitly established in CakeCraft's verified product information — never inferred from an ingredient list alone. If our current product information does not confirm a dietary property a customer is asking about, the honest answer is that our team needs to verify it before the order is confirmed, not a guess in either direction.

## Response Tone
Dietary, allergy, and religious questions should always receive a warm, sympathetic, and genuinely helpful response — never a bare refusal, never dismissive, and never alarmist. Acknowledge the customer's requirement respectfully, share what CakeCraft's verified information actually says, and explain plainly that our team will confirm the specific details before the order is confirmed.

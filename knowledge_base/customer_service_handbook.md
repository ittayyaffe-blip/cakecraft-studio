# Customer Service Handbook — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Tone
Warm, personal, and specific — customers should feel like they're talking to someone who knows their order, not reading a form letter. Reference the actual cake design, occasion, or customer name whenever replying, rather than generic phrasing.

## Response Time Expectations
- Order status inquiries: same business day.
- New order inquiries (design questions, availability): within 24 hours.
- Complaints or urgent issues (day-of pickup problems, allergy concerns): immediately, escalate to the head baker if a staff member cannot resolve it directly.

## Handling Common Situations
- **"Is my order ready?"** — check the order's current status before replying; never guess. If it's not yet ready, give the actual scheduled pickup time, not a vague "soon."
- **"Can I change my order?"** — changes to flavor/filling/frosting are usually possible up to 48 hours before the scheduled pickup; changes to size or design closer to pickup may not be accommodable since production may already be underway (see Production Workflow).
- **"I have a food allergy"** — always follow the Allergen Policy exactly; never promise an allergen-free product.
- **Complaints about cake quality** — apologize genuinely, offer a resolution (remake, partial refund, or credit toward a future order per the Pricing Policy), and document the complaint so the head baker can review what went wrong.

## VIP and Repeat Customers
Customers with a longer order history or higher lifetime value (visible in the CRM) should be acknowledged as returning customers — a returning Wedding or Corporate client, in particular, is a strong candidate for proactive outreach ahead of a likely repeat occasion (an anniversary, a recurring company event).

## What Staff Should Never Do
Never promise a delivery/pickup time, price, or allergen accommodation that conflicts with this knowledge base without checking first. It is always better to say "let me confirm and get back to you" than to overpromise and disappoint a customer later.

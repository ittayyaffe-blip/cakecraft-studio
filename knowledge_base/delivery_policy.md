# Delivery Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Service Area
Delivery is available within a 25 km radius of the bakery. Addresses beyond this radius are handled case-by-case and typically require the customer to arrange their own transport or accept a higher delivery fee to cover a longer round trip.

## Fees
- Standard delivery (0–10 km): €15 flat fee.
- Extended delivery (10–25 km): €25 flat fee.
- Wedding and multi-tier Corporate orders: delivery is included in the order's setup service (see Wedding Cake Guide and Corporate Orders Guide) rather than charged separately, since these orders require a staff member on-site for final assembly regardless.

## Scheduling
Delivery windows are booked in 2-hour blocks (e.g., 10:00 AM–12:00 PM) and must be confirmed at least 48 hours ahead of the requested slot. Same-day delivery is not offered — same-day requests should be directed to in-store pickup instead.

## Transport Standards
Cakes are transported flat, never stacked in transit, in a temperature-controlled vehicle. Tiered cakes (2+ tiers) are transported unstacked, with final on-site assembly by a decorator — this is why tiered-cake deliveries require a staff member present, not just a driver.

## Cancellations and Reschedules
A confirmed delivery can be rescheduled free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations inside 24 hours of the scheduled delivery are subject to the same policy as a pickup no-show (see Pickup Policy) since the cake is already produced by that point.

## Failed Delivery
If no one is available to receive the delivery at the scheduled address and window, the driver will wait 15 minutes, then return the cake to the bakery. The customer is responsible for arranging pickup and may be charged a redelivery fee for a second attempt.

# Wedding Cake Guide — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Why Weddings Need More Lead Time
Wedding orders require 14–45 days' notice, far more than any other collection. A wedding cake typically involves a tasting appointment, a custom sugar-flower or piping design matched to the couple's color palette, and multi-tier structural assembly — all of which need to be scheduled and approved well before the event date, not compressed into a normal 2-week production cycle.

## Process
1. **Inquiry and tasting** — the couple samples flavor/filling/frosting combinations (see Recipe Guide) and discusses design references.
2. **Design confirmation** — sugar flower color, tier count, and any custom topper are confirmed in writing before production is scheduled.
3. **Production** — tiers are baked and assembled over 2–3 days immediately before the event, with sugar flowers prepared up to a week ahead (they dry and hold color well).
4. **Delivery and on-site assembly** — nearly all wedding cakes are delivered and assembled on-site by a decorator (see Delivery Policy), not handed over pre-stacked, to avoid transit damage to a fully assembled multi-tier cake.

## Seasonality
Wedding demand is strongly seasonal, concentrated May through September. Staffing and ingredient ordering should anticipate this — a Wedding order booked in July should be treated as more likely during the summer peak, and the bakery should expect a higher concurrent Wedding order load during these months than the rest of the year.

## Pricing Structure
Wedding cakes are priced per tier plus a design complexity surcharge for sugar flowers or hand-piped detail beyond the base template. A non-refundable deposit of 30% is required at design confirmation, per the Pricing Policy, given the amount of dedicated design and ingredient-sourcing time committed before production begins.

## Common Customer Questions
- **"Can we change the guest count close to the date?"** — tier count (and therefore total servings) should be finalized at least 14 days out; late increases may not be accommodable within the remaining production window.
- **"Can the cake be delivered the morning of the wedding?"** — yes, this is the standard approach and is included in Wedding order delivery service.

# Pricing Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Base Pricing
Every cake template has a base price tied to its collection and size, shown at order time. Size selection (Small, Medium, Large) adjusts the base price via a fixed price adjustment per size — larger sizes cost more due to both ingredient volume and additional bake/decoration time.

## What's Included
Base price includes the standard flavor/filling/frosting combination shown on the template. Flavor, filling, and frosting substitutions within the standard list (see Recipe Guide) do not change the price — all listed options are priced equally.

## Surcharges
- **Custom design work** beyond a template's standard look (extra sugar flowers, hand-piped detail, edible print/logo) is quoted individually by the head baker before the order is confirmed.
- **Rush orders** placed inside a collection's normal lead-time window (see Bakery Operations Manual) may carry a rush surcharge to reflect reprioritized production.
- **Delivery** is charged per the Delivery Policy, except where included in Wedding order service.

## Deposits
- Wedding orders require a non-refundable 30% deposit at design confirmation.
- All other collections are paid in full at order confirmation or pickup/delivery — no deposit is required.

## Refunds and Credits
- A cancellation more than 7 days before the scheduled pickup/delivery is fully refundable (minus any Wedding deposit, which is non-refundable regardless of timing).
- A cancellation inside 7 days is refundable at 50%, since ingredients are typically already ordered or production may have started.
- A no-show (see Pickup Policy) is not eligible for any refund.
- Quality complaints resolved with a remake or credit are handled at the head baker's discretion, not a fixed formula.

## Currency and Payment
All prices are in the currency shown on the order form at the time of ordering. Payment is collected at pickup or delivery unless a deposit was already taken (Wedding orders) or a corporate invoice arrangement is in place (see Corporate Orders Guide).

# Business Information — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Location
Maison de Gâteau Paris (CakeCraft Studio) is located at 15 Rue de Rivoli, Paris, France. This is both the kitchen and the retail pickup counter — there is no second location.

## Phone
Customers can reach the bakery by phone at +33 1 42 56 78 90.

## Visiting or Picking Up
The retail counter and pickup window are open 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Tuesday through Sunday (closed Mondays) — see the Bakery Operations Manual for the full hours of operation and the Pickup Policy for pickup-specific details.

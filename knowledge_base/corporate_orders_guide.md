# Corporate Orders Guide — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Typical Use Cases
Corporate orders are typically for office celebrations, product launches, client appreciation events, and team milestones. They differ from other collections mainly in presentation requirements and delivery timing, not recipe.

## Lead Time and Volume
Corporate orders require 3–10 days' notice. Sheet-cake-style orders for larger headcounts (e.g., an all-staff celebration) should be flagged early since they may require a Large size or multiple cakes, which affects both bake capacity and decoration time.

## Branding and Presentation
Corporate orders frequently request a company logo or brand colors on the cake — this is accommodated via an edible print or hand-piped color match, noted in the order's custom notes. Clean, modern presentation (minimal sugar-flower work, precise color-matched fondant or buttercream) is the standard look for this collection, distinct from the more decorative Wedding or Birthday styles.

## Delivery
Corporate orders are commonly delivered to an office reception rather than picked up, and delivery is typically requested for a specific time window (e.g., "by 2pm before the afternoon meeting") — see Delivery Policy for standard windows and fees. Delivery fees for Corporate orders are not automatically waived the way Wedding delivery is; each Corporate order's delivery is quoted per the standard Delivery Policy unless otherwise negotiated.

## Invoicing
Corporate clients ordering repeatedly may request an invoice rather than paying at pickup/delivery — this should be routed to the head baker or owner, since standard order intake does not currently generate formal invoices.

## Repeat Business
Corporate clients are disproportionately likely to become repeat, higher-value customers (see CRM customer tiers) — consistent quality and reliable delivery timing on a first Corporate order matters more for long-term relationship value than for a one-off Birthday order.

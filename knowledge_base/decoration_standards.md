# Decoration Standards — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## House Style
Every cake, regardless of collection, should meet a baseline standard: clean crumb coat with no visible cake showing through, level tiers, and smooth or intentionally-textured icing (never accidentally uneven). This baseline applies before any custom design work is added.

## Collection-Specific Looks
- **Birthday** — playful and colorful; piped borders, simple lettering, often a themed topper.
- **Wedding** — elegant, restrained color palettes (ivory, gold, blush are the most requested), sugar flowers matched to the couple's palette, precise tier stacking. See Wedding Cake Guide.
- **Baby Shower** — soft pastel tones (blue, blush, or neutral for a gender-reveal cake where the interior color is the surprise), simple, delicate detail.
- **Graduation** — school colors when provided, graduation year or cap-and-diploma motifs.
- **Corporate** — clean, modern, minimal decoration; logo or brand-color accuracy matters more than decorative flourish.

## Sugar Flowers
Made in-house, dried at least 24 hours before use so they hold their shape when placed. Color-matching to a customer's palette should be tested and approved before full-batch production, especially for Wedding orders where the flowers are a focal point.

## Lettering and Custom Text
Piped lettering (e.g., "Happy Birthday!", a name, a graduation year) should be proofed against the order's notes field before piping — a spelling error in icing cannot be corrected without redoing that section of the cake.

## Quality Check Before Marking "Ready"
Before an order moves to "ready" status, the decorator should visually check: level stacking, no visible crumb coat, correct custom text/colors matching the order notes, and structural stability (no leaning tiers) — especially important for multi-tier Wedding and large Corporate cakes that will be transported.

## Weather Considerations
Buttercream and Dark Chocolate Ganache hold up best in heat; Cream Cheese frosting is the least heat-stable and should be flagged for extra care (shorter time out of refrigeration, ideally same-day pickup) on any order scheduled during a hot outdoor event.

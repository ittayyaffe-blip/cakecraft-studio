# Bakery Operations Manual — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Hours of Operation
The bakery kitchen operates Tuesday through Sunday, 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The retail counter and pickup window are open 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on the same days. The bakery is closed Mondays for deep cleaning, equipment maintenance, and staff rest.

## Daily Rhythm
Mornings (6:00–11:00 AM) are reserved for sponge and base-layer baking — every cake scheduled for same-day or next-day pickup gets its cake layers baked first thing, while the ovens are freshest and staff attention is highest. Midday (11:00 AM–3:00 PM) is filling, stacking, and crumb-coating. Afternoons (3:00–7:00 PM) are final decoration, detail work, and next-day prep (soaking, syrup-making, sugar flower work that needs overnight drying time).

## Staffing Model
A standard day runs with 2 bakers and 1 decorator. On days with predicted order volume in the "High" or "Very High" workload band, a third baker or a second decorator should be scheduled — decoration is almost always the bottleneck for complex orders (Wedding, multi-tier Corporate), not baking capacity.

## Collections
The bakery offers five cake collections, each with its own guide: Birthday, Wedding, Baby Shower, Graduation, and Corporate. Every collection has three signature templates, each independently customizable by size, flavor, filling, and frosting.

## Order Lead Times
- Birthday, Baby Shower, Graduation: 2–14 days notice.
- Corporate: 3–10 days notice.
- Wedding: 14–45 days notice (see the Wedding Cake Guide for why).

Rush orders under these windows are accommodated case-by-case at the head baker's discretion and may carry a rush surcharge — see the Pricing Policy.

## Escalation
Any order-specific question a staff member cannot answer from this knowledge base should be escalated to the head baker before making a promise to a customer, especially around allergens, custom wedding requests, or delivery logistics outside the standard service area.

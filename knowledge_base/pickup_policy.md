# Pickup Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Pickup Window
Every order has a scheduled pickup date and time selected at order confirmation. The pickup counter is open 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, Tuesday through Sunday. Cakes are ready no earlier than the scheduled time — arriving early does not guarantee an earlier handover, since decoration is often finished within the final hours before the scheduled slot.

## Identification
Customers should be prepared to state the name and phone number the order was placed under. No formal ID is required for standard orders, but for high-value Wedding orders (see Wedding Cake Guide) staff should confirm the customer's identity matches the order record before releasing the cake.

## Late Pickup
If a customer has not arrived within 2 hours of the scheduled pickup time, staff should attempt to contact them via the phone number on file. Cakes are held refrigerated for same-day late pickup. A cake not collected by closing time is held overnight and the customer contacted again the next business day; perishable fillings (Lemon Curd, Strawberry) may affect freshness after 24 hours, and the customer should be informed of this if pickup is delayed that long.

## No-Shows
An order not collected within 48 hours of its scheduled pickup, with no response to outreach, is considered a no-show. The order is marked cancelled and the cake is not held further. No-show orders are not eligible for a refund of any deposit paid, per the Pricing Policy.

## Changing Pickup Time
Pickup time changes should be requested at least 24 hours ahead where possible, since production is scheduled backward from the pickup slot (see Production Workflow). Same-day pickup-time changes may not be accommodable if decoration is already in progress for the originally scheduled time.

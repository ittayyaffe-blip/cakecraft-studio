# Food Safety Procedures — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Temperature Control
Finished cakes with dairy-based fillings or frostings (Cream Cheese, Chocolate Ganache, Strawberry with pastry cream) must be refrigerated until 2 hours before pickup/delivery at the earliest. Buttercream-frosted cakes without a dairy filling are more heat-stable and can tolerate longer at room temperature, but should still not sit unrefrigerated more than 4 hours before serving.

## Time-Out-of-Refrigeration Guidance for Customers
Staff should tell customers picking up a Cream Cheese or fresh-fruit-filled cake that it should not sit unrefrigerated more than 3 hours before serving, and should be refrigerated again promptly if not served immediately. This guidance should accompany every pickup or delivery of those specific products, not just be available on request.

## Kitchen Hygiene
Standard commercial kitchen practice applies: handwashing between tasks, color-coded tools for allergen-sensitive prep versus standard prep, and daily sanitization of all decorating surfaces. Any staff member feeling unwell should not handle food and should notify the head baker.

## Ingredient Handling
Perishable ingredients (dairy, fresh fruit, eggs) are received daily or every other day and dated on arrival; nothing is used past its labeled use-by date regardless of visual/smell inspection. Sugar flowers and fondant decorations, being shelf-stable, may be prepared several days ahead and stored separately from perishable components.

## Cross-Contact Prevention
See the Allergen Policy for the facility's shared-equipment limitations. Within that constraint, staff should still use freshly cleaned tools when a specific accommodation has been requested, and should never reuse a filling/frosting tool across a nut-garnished cake and a plain cake without cleaning in between.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected food safety issue — a customer report of illness, a temperature excursion, an ingredient past date accidentally used — must be reported to the head baker the same day, documented, and not left for the next shift to discover.

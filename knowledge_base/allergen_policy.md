# Allergen Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Facility Statement
Maison de Gâteau Paris operates a single shared kitchen. Every cake is made in a facility that also processes **wheat/gluten, eggs, dairy, tree nuts (almond, pistachio), and soy**. We do not offer a certified allergen-free environment and cannot guarantee zero cross-contact for any order, regardless of recipe.

## Standard Recipe Allergens
- All cake sponges (Vanilla, Chocolate, Red Velvet) contain **wheat, egg, and dairy**.
- Buttercream and Cream Cheese frostings contain **dairy**; Dark Chocolate Ganache contains **dairy and soy lecithin** (from the chocolate).
- Sugar flowers and fondant decorations may contain **soy** (in the fondant base) and are produced on shared decorating surfaces.
- No standard recipe currently contains tree nuts, but nut-based decorations (e.g., pistachio garnish) are available on request and are prepared on the same surfaces as nut-free cakes.

## Customer Disclosure
Every order form and pickup confirmation must ask whether any consumer has a known food allergy. If yes, staff must clearly state the facility's shared-equipment limitation before the order is confirmed — a customer with a **severe or life-threatening allergy should be advised in writing that Maison de Gâteau Paris cannot guarantee a safe product for them**, regardless of any requested substitution.

## Accommodations We Can and Cannot Make
- We **can** omit a specific ingredient (e.g., a nut garnish) or substitute a listed filling/frosting.
- We **cannot** produce a genuinely gluten-free, dairy-free, or nut-free cake, because the kitchen itself is not segregated for those allergens.
- Any customer requesting an allergen-free product should be referred, honestly, to a certified allergen-free bakery rather than accepting the order.

## Internal Handling
Staff preparing an order flagged for an allergy sensitivity (not a severe allergy) should use freshly washed tools and a cleaned section of the counter, and note the accommodation in the order's notes field for the next staff member handling it.

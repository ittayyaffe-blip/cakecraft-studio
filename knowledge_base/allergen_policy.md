# Allergen Policy — Maison de Gâteau Paris

## Facility Statement
Maison de Gâteau Paris is a 100% gluten-free bakery. Every cake is made using gluten-free ingredients and prepared in a dedicated gluten-free environment — we do not use wheat or any gluten-containing ingredient anywhere in our kitchen. Gluten-free does not mean free of all allergens: our shared kitchen still processes **milk, eggs, tree nuts (almond, pistachio), and soy**, and we do not offer a certified allergen-free environment for those — we cannot guarantee zero cross-contact with them for any order.

## Standard Recipe Allergens
- All cake sponges (Vanilla, Chocolate, Red Velvet) are made with gluten-free flour and contain **egg and dairy**.
- Buttercream and Cream Cheese frostings contain **dairy**; Dark Chocolate Ganache contains **dairy and soy lecithin** (from the chocolate).
- Sugar flowers and fondant decorations may contain **soy** (in the fondant base) and are produced on shared decorating surfaces.
- No standard recipe currently contains tree nuts, but nut-based decorations (e.g., pistachio garnish) are available on request and are prepared on the same surfaces as nut-free cakes.

## Customer Disclosure
Every order requires the customer to confirm, before submission, that they do not have a food allergy — this is a mandatory step in both the website order form and Chat-assisted ordering, and the order cannot be completed automatically without it. A customer who has a food allergy (milk, egg, tree nut, soy, or otherwise) must not complete the automated ordering process; they should be directed to contact the bakery directly so staff can discuss their requirements individually. This applies regardless of the gluten-free facility statement above, since gluten is not the only allergen present in our kitchen.

## Accommodations We Can and Cannot Make
- We **can** omit a specific ingredient (e.g., a nut garnish) or substitute a listed filling/frosting.
- We **cannot** produce a genuinely dairy-free, egg-free, or nut-free cake, because the kitchen itself is not segregated for those allergens (gluten is the one exception — see the Facility Statement above).
- Any customer requesting a dairy-free, egg-free, or nut-free product should be referred, honestly, to a bakery certified for that specific allergen rather than accepting the order.

## Internal Handling
Staff preparing an order flagged for an allergy sensitivity (not a severe allergy, and not through the automated flow — see Customer Disclosure above) should use freshly washed tools and a cleaned section of the counter, and note the accommodation in the order's notes field for the next staff member handling it.

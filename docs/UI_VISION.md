# CakeCraft Studio – UI Vision

## Design Goal

CakeCraft Studio should feel like a premium boutique bakery rather than a generic online store.

The experience should communicate elegance, warmth, craftsmanship, and celebration.

---

## Visual Style

- Modern
- Premium
- Elegant
- Minimal
- Warm
- Inviting

---

## Color Palette

Primary:
- Warm ivory
- Chocolate brown
- Rose gold

Accent:
- Soft beige
- Cream
- Light gold

Avoid:
- Bright primary colors
- Harsh blacks
- Flat gray interfaces

---

## Typography

Elegant headings.

Highly readable body text.

Generous spacing.

---

## Imagery

Large, high-quality cake photography.

Professional bakery atmosphere.

No clip art.

No cartoon graphics.

---

## Components

Large cards.

Rounded corners.

Soft shadows.

Modern buttons.

Consistent spacing.

---

## User Experience

The interface should feel calm and luxurious.

Every page should guide the customer naturally toward placing an order.

The design should prioritize clarity over visual clutter.
